<objective>
Add per-instance try-catch to the tick loops in CosmeticManager and PetManager so a single failing instance never aborts the entire loop. Also add world-null guards to CosmeticInstance.tick(), PetInstance.tick(), and HologramVisibilityTracker.update() to prevent NPE when entities are in a partially-unloaded world.

Purpose: Eliminates silent tick-loop aborts and NPEs that cause cascading cleanup failures under normal server conditions (entity death mid-tick, world unload).
Output: CosmeticManager and PetManager with Logger-injected constructors and guarded tickAll(); CosmeticInstance, PetInstance, and HologramVisibilityTracker with world-null guards.
</objective>
